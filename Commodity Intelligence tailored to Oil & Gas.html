The problem CAnalytics solves

SOCAR works across the whole value chain: Azeri Light crude exported through Baku–Tbilisi–Ceyhan, Shah Deniz gas sent to Türkiye and Europe through the Southern Gas Corridor, refining and petrochemicals at STAR and Petkim, and global trading through SOCAR Trading.

Each business produces a lot of data, but it is split across ERP, trading, logistics, finance and compliance systems, while market data (Brent, Dated differentials, TTF, freight, FX) sits in separate terminals. The questions that matter most need all of these sources at once.

  • How does our realised price for Azeri Light compare with Dated Brent, and what is driving the differential this month?
  • Which open contracts are most exposed if Brent drops 15%, or if Mediterranean freight doubles?
  • Which counterparties are starting to pay later, and how much of our receivables do they represent?
  • Is a disruption at Hormuz, in the Red Sea or the Black Sea already visible in physical flows before it shows up in the price?
  • Is a vessel nominated for our cargo linked to a sanctioned owner, or to dark-fleet activity?

Forecast corridors and anomaly detection

  • A 30-day price corridor for each benchmark and hub, produced by a modern time-series foundation model.
  • The corridor is meant for risk limits and anomaly alerts, not as a single-point price prediction, and the platform says so.
76%of outcomes inside the 80% corridor, over 12 back-tests

Early warning of physical market stress

  • A stress index built from physical signals: requests for quotes, withdrawn offers, vessels slowing at chokepoints, changes in hub premiums and market chatter in several languages.
  • In the demo environment, it flagged the 2026 Hormuz shock 5 trading days before Brent's largest move.
  • On unseen data, 4 of 8 Brent shocks were preceded by an alert (p = 0.01) with no false alarms.

Shocks driven purely by news, such as tariffs or ceasefires, are correctly not anticipated, and the platform reports this rather than hiding it.

Sanctions and dark-fleet screening

  • Vessel risk alerts from AIS gaps near sanctioned ports, ship-to-ship transfers, frequent reflagging and ownership links.
  • Each alert lists its evidence: hours dark, position, counterpart vessel, owners and designation dates, the level of detail a compliance officer, bank or insurer will accept.
  • No alarms on 36 innocent look-alikes (dry-dock repairs, legal lightering, ordinary vessel sales). Known weak spots are documented.

A knowledge graph that remembers what you knew, and when

  • Every fact about vessels, owners and counterparties is stored with two dates: when it was true and when the company learned it.
  • This answers what regulators, boards and courts ask: on the day you financed or loaded this cargo, what did you know?
  • Chains of exposure (who owes whom, and who owes them) return in milliseconds, the starting point for contagion analysis.
2 dateson every fact: valid time and known time

Local-language text intelligence, on your own servers

  • A compact classifier reads market and logistics messages for delays, force majeure and urgency, and answers “needs review” when unsure instead of guessing.
  • Runs on-premise: no text leaves SOCAR's infrastructure and there is no per-token bill to a foreign AI provider.
  • Can be fine-tuned on Azerbaijani and Turkish trade correspondence.

Bringing in SOCAR's own data

Client data plugs straight into the same analytical pipeline that runs the demo.

  1. Connect your sources

    Contracts, counterparties, nominations, shipments, invoices and payments from SAP, ETRM, spreadsheets or database exports. AIS feeds, OFAC, EU and UN sanctions lists, and prices from Platts, Argus or ICE.

  2. System of record

    A governed PostgreSQL/TimescaleDB store. Every record is tagged with its origin:

    your datamarket datalive feedforecast

  3. Analytical replica

    An automatic sync builds a ClickHouse replica with ready views for exposure, margins, routes, basis and counterparty behaviour.

  4. Insight

    The AI analyst, dashboards, forecasts, stress index and sanctions screening run on your data at once, with no separate project for each.

Correlations with world prices

Once internal and market series share one store, the platform measures how SOCAR's realised prices, volumes, margins and payment behaviour move with global benchmarks, FX and freight, including lead/lag tests that show which signals move first. Each relationship is tested statistically, so correlations that happen by chance are not presented as insight.

Why CAnalytics is different

  • Physical flow, not only pricesEveryone has price screens. CAnalytics adds the order flow, vessel movements and correspondence that move first.
  • Every number can be checkedEach answer shows its method, its SQL and its data origin.
  • Honest about limitsAccuracy is measured on held-out data and failures are published. Risk signals are labelled as signals, not proven violations.
  • Fast to deployThe full pipeline already works end to end. A client deployment mostly means connecting data sources and adding company-specific views.
